Compression report MVP mockup · v1

First-release slice: arrivals and departures by day, plus hourly demand as stacked bars (aircraft-category mix by hour) for a sample selected day.

What ships in v1 (this mockup)
  • Location + date range; always shows both arrivals and departures.
  • Daily totals matrix plus hourly stacked bar chart by aircraft category.
  • One export action (CSV)—PDF, saved views, and extra filters deferred.
Defer to later
  • Movement / aircraft / leg-status / customer filters; optional hour grid layout.
  • Clickable matrix column drill UX (sample day stays static until then).
Filters

Additional filters listed in the full mockup (flightbridge-compression-report-mockup.html) are intentionally omitted for MVP.
