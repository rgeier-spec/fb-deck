Compression report MVP mockup · v1

First-release slice: arrivals and departures by day with a fixed hourly breakdown for one selected example day. Scoped to trips visible for your FlightBridge login.

What ships in v1 (this mockup)
  • Location + date range; always shows both arrivals and departures.
  • Daily totals matrix and hourly counts (single grid layout only).
  • One export action (CSV)—PDF, saved views, and extra filters deferred.
Defer to later
  • Movement / aircraft / leg-status / customer filters; stacked bar hourly view.
  • Aircraft mix table under hourly; clickable column drill UX.
Filters

Additional filters listed in the full mockup (flightbridge-compression-report-mockup.html) are intentionally omitted for MVP.
